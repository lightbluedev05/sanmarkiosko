# Sanmarkiosko Product Context

## Users
University students at a campus micro-economy. They are both buyers and sellers of products and services.

## Product Purpose
Connect students to facilitate on-campus commerce (food, academic help, tech services, etc.) in a safe and easy-to-use marketplace.

## Brand & Tone
- **Tone:** Reliable, modern, helpful, academic yet vibrant.
- **Brand Colors:** Deep university blue or maroon (primary), plenty of white space.
- **Language:** Strictly Spanish (ES).
- **Vibe:** Clean, no visual clutter, "alive" and polished.

## Strategic Principles
- **Mobile-First:** Primary usage is on-the-go via mobile devices.
- **Trust:** Verified "Pro Sellers" and clear categorization.
- **Engagement:** Promoted listings to drive micro-monetization.

## Register
product

## Anti-References
- Overly cluttered marketplaces (e.g., old eBay).
- Generic corporate dashboards.
- Non-responsive designs.
